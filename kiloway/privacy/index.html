Privacy Policy for Kiloway

Effective date: November 1, 2026

Kiloway ("the app") helps you log meals by chatting with an AI assistant and keep track of your weight. This policy explains what data the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have.

The short version: almost everything stays on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. The only things you write or create that leave your device are your meal chat and the photos you choose to send to the AI assistant, and only after you agree to it, plus any feedback you choose to send us.

Who we are

Kiloway is made by Yiwei Miao ("we", "us"), who is responsible for the processing of personal data described in this policy (the "controller" under the GDPR). If you have any questions about this policy or your data, contact us at hello.alexmiao@gmail.com.

Data stored only on your device

The following data is stored only on your iPhone, in the app's local storage, protected by iOS data protection. We never receive it:

  • Your profile: your name or nickname, age, sex, height, goal, goal weight, activity level, and pace.
  • Your profile picture: the symbol you pick, or the photo you choose from your library, saved as a small image inside the app.
  • Your targets: daily calorie and macronutrient goals.
  • Your logs: meals, weights, goal journeys, and your meal chat history, including the photos you sent in the chat.
  • Your settings, such as units, appearance, chat language, reminders, and whether AI meal logging is turned on.

The app has no cloud sync and no account, so this data isn't copied to other devices. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud Backup or to a computer, the app's data is included in that backup, like the data of other apps. Apple stores iCloud backups under your Apple ID; we can't access them.

Camera and photos

  • The app uses the camera only when you tap Camera to take a photo of a meal.
  • When you choose photos, the app uses the iOS photo picker, so it receives only the photos you select and has no access to the rest of your library.
  • Before a meal photo is sent, the app resizes it and removes its metadata, such as location and camera details. The app never saves photos to your photo library.
  • Please photograph only your food. Avoid including people, documents, or anything else you don't want to share.

Data sent to the AI service

AI meal logging is optional and only works after you agree to it, during setup or later in Settings. When you send a message in the meal chat, the app sends the following to our server so the AI can analyze it:

  • your message and any photos attached to it (up to 4, resized and with metadata removed),
  • today's earlier chat messages (your last 20 messages and the replies in between). Photos from earlier messages are not sent again; only the number of photos is mentioned,
  • today's food entries (food names, portions, calories, macronutrients, and the time they were logged),
  • your current daily calorie and macronutrient targets and how much of them is left today,
  • your unit setting (metric or imperial), your device's language, the chat language if you chose one, and your local time.

When you add food to a past day from that day's chat, the app sends that day's earlier chat messages and food entries, what was left of your targets that day, and the date of that day, instead of today's. Messages written while you're offline are sent the same way once you're back online.

The app never sends your name, profile picture, age, sex, height, weight entries, goal journeys, or any Apple Health data. If you write your weight in a message yourself (for example, "70.5 today"), it's sent as part of that message.

How it's processed: Our server runs on Supabase in the European Union (Ireland). It forwards the request to Anthropic, which provides the AI model, and returns the result to your device. Our server does not store your messages, photos, or food entries. Our server logs record only technical details, such as the response status, response time, the number of AI tokens used, the number of entries returned, and the number and total size of photos. They never include the text of your messages or the content of your photos.

For each reply, our server also adds one to an anonymous daily count of the kind of request, such as logging food or asking a nutrition question, and whether it included a photo. These counts contain no message content and no identifier, and can't be linked to you.

Anthropic processes the request only to produce the response, as our processor under its Commercial Terms and Data Processing Addendum. Under these terms, Anthropic may not use your messages or photos to train its models. Anthropic automatically deletes inputs and outputs, including photos, within 30 days. It may keep them longer only where needed to enforce its usage policy (requests flagged by its safety systems are kept for up to 2 years) or where required by law. Our contract is with Anthropic Ireland Limited, but Anthropic may process the data outside the European Union, including in the United States. These transfers are protected by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK Addendum) in Anthropic's Data Processing Addendum.

You can turn AI meal logging off at any time in Settings. After that, nothing from your chat is sent. You can still add and edit entries by hand.

Translated app messages

If your iPhone isn't set to English, the app downloads translations of its built-in messages (such as morning messages and meal reminders) from our server, which translates them with the same AI service. The request contains only the language, a version number, and the random identifier described under "Usage limits". It never contains your messages, logs, or profile.

Notifications

Morning messages and lunch and dinner reminders are local notifications. The app creates them on your iPhone from your own data and schedules them there; we don't send push notifications and don't receive this data. Notifications on the lock screen don't show calorie numbers. You can turn each of them off in Settings.

Subscription

AI meal logging needs a subscription after your free messages. Purchases, free trials, and payments are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store, under Apple's own terms and privacy policy. We don't receive your payment details or your Apple ID. The app checks your subscription status on your iPhone through Apple's StoreKit and doesn't send it to our server. The number of free messages you've used is stored only on your iPhone, in the Keychain.

Usage limits

To prevent abuse and keep costs under control, our server limits how many requests can be made per day. To do this, the app creates a random identifier that is stored in your device's Keychain. This identifier is not linked to your name, Apple ID, or any other identifier. It is sent with each AI, translation, and feedback request, together with your IP address, which every internet request includes.

Our server never stores this identifier or your IP address in readable form. It stores only a salted one-way hash of each, together with daily request counts, and deletes these records automatically after two days. We also keep anonymous daily totals for each day, such as how many devices used the assistant and how many reached the daily limit. These totals contain no identifier, hash, IP address, or message content and can't be linked to you.

Supabase, our hosting provider, keeps standard technical request logs, which can include IP addresses, for a short period set by our plan (currently one day). Supabase acts as our processor under its Data Processing Addendum. Our server and database are located in the European Union; where Supabase, a US company, accesses data from outside the EU, the EU Standard Contractual Clauses in that addendum apply.

The random identifier and the free-message count are not reset when you delete your data in the app or delete the app, so the limits can't be bypassed.

Feedback

You can send us feedback through the optional form in the app. It doesn't go through the AI service. We store your answers, your optional comment, the date (without the time), and the app version in our database in the European Union. We don't store your device identifier, IP address, or anything else that could link the feedback to you or your device. Please don't include personal information in your comment.

Feedback is kept for 12 months and then deleted. Because it can't be linked to you, we can't find or delete a specific person's feedback, and "Delete all data" in the app doesn't remove feedback you've already sent.

Sending feedback may give you extra AI messages for that day. To do this, our server adds them to the daily usage record of your device (see "Usage limits"), which is kept separately from your feedback and can't be linked to it.

Screenshots: You can attach up to 3 screenshots to your feedback. Screenshots can show health data, such as your weight and meals, so the app sends them only if you tick the consent box each time. Before sending, the app removes their metadata. Screenshots are stored in private storage in the European Union, used only to improve the app, never sent to the AI service, and deleted automatically after 90 days. Like the rest of your feedback, they can't be linked to you, so we can't find or delete them on request.

Apple Health

If you turn on "Read weight from Apple Health" in Settings, the app asks for permission to read your weight from Apple Health. The app:

  • only reads weight, and never writes anything to Apple Health;
  • copies the weights into its local storage on your device, only to show your weight history and trend;
  • never sends Apple Health data to our server or to anyone else;
  • never uses Apple Health data for advertising, marketing, or data mining, and never sells it;
  • never syncs Apple Health data to iCloud. (Like the rest of the app's local data, the copied weights are included in your own iPhone backup if you use one; see "Data stored only on your device".)

You can turn this off in Settings, or remove access in the Health app under Sharing > Apps.

Legal basis (EU and UK users)

  • AI meal analysis (messages and photos): your explicit consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and 9(2)(a)), which you give by agreeing to AI meal logging. Meal information can reveal information about your health, so we ask for your consent before any of it is sent. You can withdraw consent at any time by turning AI meal logging off.
  • Feedback: your consent, which you give by sending the form; for screenshots, your explicit consent each time (Art. 9(2)(a)).
  • Usage limits, server logs, and the logs of this website: our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure, preventing abuse, and controlling costs (Art. 6(1)(f)).

How long data is kept

  • On your device: until you delete it or delete the app.
  • Meal messages and photos: not stored on our server; deleted by Anthropic within 30 days, except as described above.
  • Usage-limit records (hashes and counts): deleted after two days.
  • Supabase's technical request logs: currently one day.
  • Feedback: 12 months; screenshots 90 days.
  • Anonymous daily totals: kept to understand how the app is used; they can't be linked to anyone.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your data: Settings > Delete all data removes your meals, chats and their photos, weights, goal journeys, profile (including your name and profile picture), settings, and consent from the device. Deleting the app also removes them. It doesn't affect your data in Apple Health, your subscription (which belongs to your Apple ID), the random identifier and free-message count in the Keychain, or feedback you've already sent.
  • Withdraw consent: turn off AI meal logging or Apple Health access at any time.
  • Cancel your subscription: in your iPhone's Settings, under your name > Subscriptions.
  • Your rights: depending on where you live, including under the GDPR, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to processing, to data portability, and to complain to your local data protection authority. We don't keep your data on our server, so in most cases there is nothing for us to access or delete. If you have a request, contact us at hello.alexmiao@gmail.com.
